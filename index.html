    ⚗️ How to use the Chemistry Lab

    • Hover any element to see its details in the big card.
    • Click an element to open the atom explorer. Choose Visual form to reveal the nucleus, shells, electron motion, and probability cloud step by step.
    • Shift-click (or ⌘/Ctrl-click) to select multiple elements — they glow.
    • Drag selected elements into the cauldron, or press Mix it!
    • Slide the temperature to heat or freeze the mix — some reactions only happen when hot.
    • Add Air, Water or a Spark. The result explains whether a fast reaction is modeled, and why.
    • Recolour the table by state of matter at your chosen temperature, electronegativity, or mass.
    • Available reactions can show standard-state thermodynamics — ΔH, ΔS and ΔG with an energy diagram. A ΔG sign does not tell you the reaction rate.
    • Explore the molecular Lewis structures tool to see clear bonds and lone pairs for common covalent molecules.
    • Stuck? The 💡 Next hint suggests a useful next condition to try.
    • Make a new compound and it joins your Discoveries list. How many can you find?

    Tip: compare Na + Cl before and after a spark, then try K + Water. The lab distinguishes a possible hazard from a confirmed explosion.
